Privacy Policy

This page explains what information PuppyMatchPro (puppymatchpro.com) collects when you visit this site or buy a product, how it's used, and how to opt out of promotional emails.

Information we collect

When you purchase a product, our payment processor Digistore24 collects your name, email address, and payment details in order to process the transaction and deliver your product. We receive your order details (name, email, and what you purchased) from Digistore24, but we never see or store your full payment/card information — that stays with Digistore24.

This site also uses standard tracking pixels (Google Ads and Pinterest) and Digistore24's affiliate-tracking script to measure which pages and campaigns lead to a sale.

How we use your information

We use your order information to deliver your purchase, respond to support requests, and process refunds. From time to time, we may also send you promotional emails about new guides or related offers.

Opting out of promotional emails

You can unsubscribe from promotional emails and newsletters at any time by clicking the "unsubscribe" link included at the bottom of any promotional email we send. You can also opt out by emailing puppymatchpro@gmail.com with "unsubscribe" in the subject line, and we'll remove you promptly.

Opting out of promotional emails does not affect transactional emails related to an active purchase — such as your download link, receipt, or order support — which we'll continue to send as needed to fulfill your order.

Cookies and tracking

This site uses cookies from Google, Pinterest, and Digistore24 for advertising measurement and affiliate attribution. You can control or block cookies through your browser's privacy settings.

Contact

Questions about this policy or your information can be sent to puppymatchpro@gmail.com.
