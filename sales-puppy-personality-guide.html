Most people choose a puppy the same way — they walk in, one little furball makes a beeline for them, melts their heart on the spot, and the decision is made right there. No research. No assessment. Just pure, unfiltered emotion.

And sometimes it works out beautifully. Other times... well. You end up with a 124-pound Alaskan Malamute who has declared himself the undisputed ruler of your street, terrorized your neighbors into threatening lawsuits, and who no trainer on earth seems able to reach for longer than three weeks.

That was my first dog. And he taught me everything I'm about to share with you.

Here's what most people never realize: by the time a puppy is just seven weeks old, its adult personality is already fully formed — and already showing itself, if you know what to look for. The dominance, the sociability, the trainability, the temperament — it's all there, right in front of you.

This guide gives you the tools to read it.

Ten simple assessments. A straightforward scoring system. And suddenly you can tell, with remarkable accuracy, whether that adorable little bundle is going to grow into a dog that's a joy to live with — or one that's going to test every ounce of your patience for the next twelve years.

This guide walks you through every step of the test, what each response means, and exactly how to match the right puppy to your personality, your household and your lifestyle. Whether you have kids, whether you're a first-time owner, whether you want a companion or a working dog — there's a right puppy in that litter for you.
